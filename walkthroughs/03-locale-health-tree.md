# Read Locale Health

Open the i18ntk Workbench Activity Bar view to review:

- Actions for scan, validate, usage analysis, and auto-translate
- Project Health totals and health score
- Missing Keys grouped by locale
- Placeholder Mismatches such as `{{name}}` drift between locales
- Unused Keys that are present in locale files but not referenced in source
- Reports for the full scan summary

[Refresh Locale Health](command:i18ntk.refreshLocaleHealth)

[Open Summary Report](command:i18ntk.openReport)
